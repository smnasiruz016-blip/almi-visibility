FAQ

Who accepts OET for Nursing?
OET's official list shows 469 organisations recognising OET for Nursing across 17 countries — regulators, boards, councils and employers. Always confirm the exact requirement with your own regulator.
What OET grade do I need for Nursing?
Most regulators ask for Grade B (350) in each sub-test; some accept C+ (300) or set their own per-sub-test rules. Always confirm the exact requirement with the organisation you're applying to.
Which professions does AlmiOET cover?
All 12 OET professions — Nursing, Medicine, Dentistry, Dietetics, Occupational Therapy, Optometry, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Podiatry, Radiography, Speech Pathology, Veterinary Science. Writing and Speaking are tailored to your profession; Listening and Reading are shared.
Is AlmiOET affiliated with OET?
No. AlmiOET is independent and not affiliated with OET or CBLA. All practice material is original and never copied from the Occupational English Test.
