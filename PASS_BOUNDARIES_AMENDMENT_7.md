## 1 · WHAT IS SUPERSEDED

Three readings, and only these three:

1. the universal minimum of **≥5 verified sourced facts** per page;
2. the universal minimum of **≥350 unique words** per page;
3. **automatic rejection solely because textual sibling overlap exceeds 40%**.

Everything else in amendment 5 §5 stands, including that insufficient support is a **DATA GAP, never padding**.

## 2 · WHAT REPLACES THEM

**A · FACT SUFFICIENCY.** No universal minimum verified-fact count. Every material factual claim actually made must be
supported by a fresh-enough approved source. A zero-fact page may pass only if it contains no unsupported material
factual claim. A factual page may never invent, infer or pad claims to satisfy a count. Missing support for a material
factual claim is DATA GAP and fails acceptance.

**B · CONTENT COMPLETENESS.** No universal minimum and no hard maximum word count. The page must completely answer one
coherent intent without filler. Missing required answer coverage fails. Text added only to reach a length is itself a
failure. Length alone never authorises splitting one intent across URLs.

**C · OVERLAP AND DUPLICATION.** Textual sibling overlap above 40% triggers MANDATORY REVIEW; it is not automatic
rejection by percentage alone. The final judgement compares intent · answer · facts · headings and architecture ·
examples · user value. Duplicate intent or value fails even when wording differs. High textual overlap may pass only
where distinct user value is MEASURED AND RECORDED.

**D · RIGHT TO EXIST.** A specific `WHY_THIS_URL_DESERVES_TO_EXIST` remains mandatory, unchanged.

**E · FAIL-CLOSED CONSTRUCTION.** Row 61 must still prove Gate A is invoked inside the construction path and fails
closed. A candidate must not be emitted as an accepted built page when a material factual claim lacks approved support ·
the coherent intent is incompletely answered · filler simulates completeness · semantic duplication lacks distinct user
value · `WHY_THIS_URL_DESERVES_TO_EXIST` is absent or insufficient · any applicable mandatory gate is bypassed.

## 4 · WHAT THE NUMERIC FLOOR CAUGHT, AND WHAT IS LOST

A replacement that is not measured against what it replaces is a weakening with better manners. Recorded here so it can
never be discovered later:

| the old floor caught | the amended rule | still caught? |
|---|---|---|
| a page with fewer than 5 facts | every cited claim must resolve to a fresh approved fact | **stricter** — one unsupported claim now fails a page that 9 supported claims used to carry |
| a page with 4 supported claims | no count | **LOST BY DESIGN** — a completely supported 1-claim page now passes |
| a thin page under 350 words | every declared section must be answered, and no two may be identical | **partly** — a page answering every declared section in few words now passes |
| a page with no declared coverage | cannot be judged → NOT TESTED → refused | **stricter** — the old floor would have passed it on word count alone |
