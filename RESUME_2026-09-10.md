## STANDING RULES IN FORCE

- **After every merge, verify on `origin/main`** — not the branch. A merged PR is a snapshot;
  pushing to the branch afterwards does nothing.
- **A gate may not build its denominator out of its own output.** "Every sibling" means every
  sibling a reader can still reach.
- **A threshold carries its sample.** `WARMUP_CEILING = 4` (n=3), `SHELL_DOC_FREQUENCY = 0.98`
  (provisional), `FACT_FRESHNESS_DAYS = 180` (provisional), A1's 25% (n=8, ESTIMATE), the
  150–205 acquisition range (**n=1 profession**).
- **Read ANY product; change NONE.** (`SCOPE_LAW.md`)
- **A source can be readable and still un-quotable.** `sourceMachineReadable` and
  `sourceQuotable` are independent fields, and OET is the case that proves it.
- **A refusal is recorded, never routed around.** No user-agent spoofing, no proxy.
- **Do not amend a frozen contract to fit a measurement** until the contract's own words have
  been read against that measurement. (`§5A.1` needed no change — the hybrid already satisfies
  *"not EVERY record"*.)
- **Still forbidden:** no page created, no page deleted, no DB table before the Neon branch
  (**stop and say so** before the first migration), no production write, no touching the other
  products.
