# THE LAST HONEST ATTEMPT — do origin claims of a DIFFERENT SHAPE exist?

**10 September 2026.** O5 and O6 verified for **two deliberately different countries** —
India, and Nigeria as the contrast (Philippines checked as a third). Sources fetched and
read. No page created, no page deleted, no production write.

> The door on 2,292 corridor pages was not going to close without this. The previous
> finding was that four of six origin claims were the same yes/no question. If claims of a
> genuinely different shape exist in numbers, the arithmetic changes and so does the
> ruling.

## 🔴 THE HEADLINE, BEFORE THE DETAIL

**They exist, and my previous characterisation understated them.** O5 is **not** a yes/no —
for Nigeria it is a named body, four separate fees, two document lists and a **UK-specific
variant**. But the conclusion does not flip, because the binding constraint turned out not
to be the shape. **It is availability**, and availability varies wildly by country.

---

## 1 · O5 — who verifies a nurse's registration, and what it costs

### 1.1 · First, a finding about the DESTINATION that changes where O5 has to come from

The NMC **publishes no per-country regulator list.** It asks the applicant to supply the
details, and says that for some countries there may be no regulator at all — in which case
it may be the institution or the Ministry of Health.

> **So there is no tier-1 destination source for O5.** The source has to be each origin
> country's own regulator, one at a time. That is a real cost: 191 origins means 191
> separate tier-1 sources, not one page with a table on it.

### 1.2 · 🇳🇬 NIGERIA — verified, and genuinely rich

**Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria**, `nmcn.gov.ng/verify.html`, fetched and read
10 September 2026. Verbatim:

| what | requirement |
|---|---|
| **Verification of certificates** | *"Receipt of payment of **N66,875** to Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria through REMITA"* + application letter stating purpose and destination, completed verification form, photocopy of current licence, photocopies of certificates of registration, photocopy of birth certificate or declaration of age |
| **Authentication** | *"Receipt of payment of **N8,750** … through REMITA(TSA)"* + two copies of the above |
| **Letter of Good Standing** | *"Original evidence of payment of **N8,750** … through REMITA (TSA)"* + the verification requirements |
| 🔴 **Verification and Letter of Good Standing to NMC-UK** | its **own** line, *"Receipt of payment of **N17,500**"* and its own document list |

**This is not a yes/no.** It is a named body, four fees, two document lists, and a
**corridor-specific variant** — Nigeria→UK has a different price and a different process
from Nigeria→anywhere. That is exactly the shape the corridor idea needed to exist.

### 1.3 · 🇮🇳 INDIA — and here O5 is not a value at all

**India has no single answer.** Verification for nurses going abroad is issued by the
**State Nursing Councils** — Kerala, Karnataka, Delhi, Tamil Nadu and the rest — not by the
Indian Nursing Council centrally. The INC's own site does not publish a going-abroad
verification process; its equivalency page addresses foreign nurses coming *into* India.

> 🔴 **For India, O5 is a BRANCH, not a fact.** The answer is "whichever of ~30 state
> councils you registered with", and the fee, form and processing time differ between them.
> A page called `/nursing/from-india` cannot state one answer without knowing the reader's
> state — and if it lists all thirty it is back to being a table.

⚠️ **Status: this is a NEGATIVE finding, and it is weaker evidence than Nigeria's.** I
could not find a tier-1 Indian source stating the division of responsibility; the sources
that describe it are agency and consultancy sites, which are **tier 4 — a lead, never a
citation.** What would close it: read two state councils' own pages and the INC's own
registration section. **Recorded as CANDIDATE, not verified.**

### 1.4 · 🇵🇭 PHILIPPINES — a single national body, and unfetchable

The **Professional Regulation Commission** issues Certification of Good Standing /
Verification centrally — one national body, unlike India. `prc.gov.ph` returned **HTTP 403**
to our fetcher, so the fees and document list are **CANDIDATE, not verified.**

## 2 · O6 — is the country's nursing education "taught and examined in English"?

**Not answerable per country from a destination source.** The NMC's own route requires a
**personal transcript** showing *"you spent at least half of your time interacting with
patients … and that at least 75% of these interactions were in English"* — i.e. the
evidence is **per applicant**, not per country. The country-level list the NMC does publish
is a PDF whose text could not be parsed.

> **O6 is not a corridor fact. It is an applicant fact.** A corridor page can explain the
> rule, but the rule is the same rule on all 191 pages — it is destination-scoped after all.
> **My earlier classification of O6 as origin-scoped was wrong, and this corrects it.**

## 3 · 🔴 A CLAIM WE HAD NOT FOUND — and it is the strongest per-origin fact yet

**The UK Code of Practice for international recruitment**, drawn from the WHO Health
Workforce Support and Safeguards List 2023.

| list | meaning | countries |
|---|---|---|
| **RED** | *"should not be targeted when actively recruiting health or care professionals"* | **54**, including **Nigeria**, **Pakistan**, Ghana, Bangladesh, Zimbabwe, Zambia, Uganda, Sudan, South Sudan, Gambia, Guinea, Equatorial Guinea |
| **AMBER** | *"international recruitment is only permitted in compliance with the terms of the government-to-government agreements in place for these countries"* | **2** — Kenya and Nepal |
| green | not listed; active recruitment permitted | everyone else, incl. **India** and the **Philippines** |

Also verbatim: a red-list country moves to amber when a government-to-government agreement
is signed; and an employer *"may consider direct applications from an individual resident in
a country on the red list if that individual is making a direct application on their own
behalf and not using a third party."*

**This genuinely changes the answer per origin, and it has three states rather than two** —
plus, for amber, the terms of a specific bilateral agreement. **It is also the single most
consequential thing a nurse from Nigeria or Pakistan could read**, and neither NMC nor OET
puts it on a page about them.

⚠️ **Honest about what it still is:** structurally it is list membership. Richer than O1's
yes/no, but the same family.

## 4 · SO — HOW MANY CLAIMS OF THIS SHAPE COULD EXIST?

The shape is: **what the ORIGIN's own institutions require of a nurse leaving for the UK,
where the answer is a value rather than a list-membership bit.**

| # | candidate claim | evidence it exists |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | which body issues verification | ✅ Nigeria (NMCN); 🟡 India (branched); 🟡 Philippines (PRC) |
| 2 | the verification fee | ✅ Nigeria ₦66,875 |
| 3 | the **destination-specific** fee | ✅ Nigeria ₦17,500 for NMC-UK |
| 4 | the document list | ✅ Nigeria, six items |
| 5 | letter of good standing — separate process and fee | ✅ Nigeria ₦8,750 |
| 6 | authentication — separate process and fee | ✅ Nigeria ₦8,750 |
| 7 | processing time | 🟡 not found at tier 1 for any of the three |
| 8 | emigration clearance / exit requirements (India eMigrate/ECR, Philippines DMW) | 🟡 plausible, unverified |

**Estimate: 6–10 genuinely per-country claims per corridor, where the origin's regulator
publishes them.** The justification is the table: six are demonstrated for one country from
a single page, and two more are plausible from a named mechanism. It is an **ESTIMATE from
n = 1 country** and it should be read as an order of magnitude, not a count.

### 4.1 · Now put it against the arithmetic

| | |
|---|---|
| words of genuinely distinct text needed to clear overlap | **~1,081 per page** (measured) |
| 6–10 per-country facts at the measured ~100 prose words each | **600 – 1,000 words** |
| **verdict** | **approaches the bar. Probably does not clear it.** |

**And the combination that DOES work is now visible, because both halves were measured:**

> **Remove the shared 469-organisation list (2,118 of 2,220 residual words) AND write 6–10
> per-country facts.** The removal collapses the shared shingles; the facts supply the
> ~600–1,000 words the page then needs to exist at all. **Neither half works alone** — the
> facts alone leave overlap at 0.97 after rollout, and the removal alone drops the page
> under the word bar.

### 4.2 · 🔴 BUT THE BINDING CONSTRAINT IS NOT THE SHAPE. IT IS AVAILABILITY.

| country | O5 available? |
|---|---|
| Nigeria | ✅ rich, published, fetchable, UK-specific |
| Philippines | 🟡 exists centrally, **403 to our fetcher** |
| India | 🔴 **no single answer** — ~30 state councils |

**Three countries, three different availability states, and India — the largest single
corridor in the network — is the worst of them.** There is no reason to expect the other
188 to be better than this sample, and several to be worse (countries the NMC itself says
may have no regulator at all).

## 5 · THE ANSWER, AS IT CAME

**1. Are these claims genuinely different per country, or another yes/no?**
**Genuinely different — I understated them before.** O5 is a body, fees, forms and a
destination-specific variant. O7 has three states plus per-country agreement terms. Only
O1–O4 are the flat yes/no. **O6 was misclassified by me and is destination-scoped.**

**2. How many claims of this shape could exist?**
**6–10 per corridor**, estimated from one country's regulator, where that regulator
publishes them.

**3. Does the arithmetic change?**
**Partly, and honestly it changes in the corridor's favour — but not to 2,292 pages.**
6–10 rich facts get close to the 1,081-word bar and clear it comfortably **if** the shared
organisation list is also removed. What does not change is that this is available for a
country like Nigeria, unavailable behind a 403 for the Philippines, and **structurally
absent for India**.

### 🔴 So the door does not slam. It narrows — to exactly the shape already ruled.

> *"Corridor safha sirf wahan qabil-e-difa hay jahan origin waqai jawab BADAL de."*

**That ruling is now measured rather than asserted.** The corridors where the origin
genuinely changes the answer are the ones whose **own regulator publishes a fee, a form and
a destination-specific process** — Nigeria is one, and the red-list countries are the ones
where the answer changes most. **That is a few dozen corridors, not 2,292**, and it is the
same order of magnitude the owner's target shape already reached from the other direction.

**And what would overturn THIS:** find a tier-1 Indian source that gives one national
answer for O5, or find that 100+ of the 191 origins publish Nigeria-grade detail. Both are
cheap to test — **read five more regulators' own sites** — and neither has been done.
