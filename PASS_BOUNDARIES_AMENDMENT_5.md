## 5 · GATE A BECOMES A REAL HARD GATE — all four parts

As ruled: ≥350 unique words after shared-shell subtraction · ≥5 verified sourced facts with usable
provenance · ≤40% sibling overlap against every applicable sibling in the template family · **a specific
`WHY_THIS_URL_DESERVES_TO_EXIST`**. Failure = the candidate is not accepted. Insufficient facts = DATA
GAP, never padding, never a lowered threshold.

### 🔁 SUPERSEDED — and beta-g was wrong here

The earlier draft of this amendment made the right-to-exist field **"a slot, declared but never judged,"**
reasoning that judging it is row 21 and row 21 is DEFERRED. **The owner overruled it, and the record shows
he is right:** the right-to-exist gate is **part of Gate A's own frozen four**, not part of row 21, and its
weakness is already a recorded defect —

> **`GATE-4` — §4's URL-justification gate asks for a SPECIFIC rationale; the check accepts ANY non-empty
> string.**

So this is closing a known defect in a gate already in scope, not building a deferred row.

**What can be enforced now, honestly:** the rationale must exist, must name a distinct human need, must not
be a template with one variable swapped, and must not be identical or near-identical to any sibling's.
Those are measurable. **Judging true specificity beyond that is not**, and the residue of `GATE-4` stays
open and is recorded as such rather than reported closed.
