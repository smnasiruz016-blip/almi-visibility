FAQ

How many organisations accept OET for Nursing?
OET's official list shows 469 recognising organisations for Nursing across regulators, boards, credentialing agencies and employers. Each sets its own grade — confirm with the organisation.
What grade do Nursing candidates need?
Most regulators ask for Grade B (350) in each sub-test; some accept C+ (300) or set their own per-sub-test rules. Always confirm the exact requirement with the organisation you're applying to.
Does OET registration replace a visa for someone from India?
No. OET supports professional registration; a visa is a separate process. Check both for your route.
