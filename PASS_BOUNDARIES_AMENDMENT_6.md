It ticks nothing. **A RULING IS NOT A TICK.** Under **RR-52 Ruling 0.3** the whole-row status of a

**cost**, which is applicable, unsatisfied and **not deferred by any frozen source**. Neither row
improves by being split. It does not move row 4, row 9 or row 58. It does not expand v0.1 scope,
authorise publishing, create evidence, or make a connected-product repository write lawful.
