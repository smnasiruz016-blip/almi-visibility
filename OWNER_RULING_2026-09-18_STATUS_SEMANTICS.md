🔴 **OWNER RULING 0 — NO IMPLICIT MIXED STATUS.**

2. A row must not be marked `⏭` because one limb is deferral-eligible while another **applicable** v0.1 limb is

   sub-proposition does **not** erase an unsatisfied applicable one, and does not improve the row.
4. Any split must be explicit enough that a later auditor can tell apart: the applicable v0.1 limb · the deferred or
   N-A limb · the whole-row terminal status.
