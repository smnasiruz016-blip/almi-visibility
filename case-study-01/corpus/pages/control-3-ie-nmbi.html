Nursing OET requirement for Nursing and Midwifery Board of Ireland (NMBI) — from Bhutan

Coming from Bhutan, your professional registration and your visa are two separate steps. OET evidence supports registration; check the grade each organisation asks for before you sit the test.

Organisation

Nursing and Midwifery Board of Ireland (NMBI)

Healthcare regulator · Ireland

Required OET grade

Listening B · Reading B · Writing B · Speaking B

Per sub-test on the 0–500 scale. Practice estimate only.

If you trained in Bhutan and are pursuing nursing registration recognised by Nursing and Midwifery Board of Ireland (NMBI), OET is the healthcare-specific English test built for clinical communication — referral letters, patient consultations, and workplace listening and reading.

Most regulators ask for Grade B (350) in each sub-test; some accept C+ (300) or set their own per-sub-test rules. Always confirm the exact requirement with the organisation you're applying to.

Official organisation page: www.nmbi.ie/Registration/Qualified-outside-the-EU/Application-Process/English-Language-Requirements

FAQ

Does Nursing and Midwifery Board of Ireland (NMBI) accept OET for Nursing?
Yes — Nursing and Midwifery Board of Ireland (NMBI) is on OET's official list of recognising organisations for Nursing. The published requirement is Listening B · Reading B · Writing B · Speaking B. Always check the current requirement on the organisation's own page.
What OET grade do I need for Nursing and Midwifery Board of Ireland (NMBI)?
OET lists Listening B · Reading B · Writing B · Speaking B for Nursing and Midwifery Board of Ireland (NMBI). Most regulators ask for Grade B (350) in each sub-test; some accept C+ (300) or set their own per-sub-test rules. Always confirm the exact requirement with the organisation you're applying to.
Is OET registration the same as a visa?
No. OET evidence supports professional registration with a regulator or body like Nursing and Midwifery Board of Ireland (NMBI). A visa is a separate step. Confirm both routes for your situation.
